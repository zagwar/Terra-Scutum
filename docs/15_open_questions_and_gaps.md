### 17.3 Technical prep
- [x] **Tiny provinces (found in F01; decided and built in F02):** four provinces were smaller than the map data's precision, so they could not be seen or clicked. Designer decision: **Vatican City, Gibraltar and the British Indian Ocean Territory are enlarged** to a small disc (10 km radius **[DEFAULT]**) at their real place, as Paradox games do with microstates, and keep their real area in the data; **Rose Atoll is left out** of the game. The list is `packages/tools/map-config/tiny-provinces.json`, and the import warns if another province ever comes out without a visible shape. The label points (`center`) that lay outside their province are fixed too: every province's label point is now the point of its shape farthest from its outline.
- [ ] **Data file schemas:** the exact fields for each JSON data file (regions, countries, provinces, sea zones, buildings, goods, techs, units, bugs, events). F07: `goods.json` and `buildings.json`, in `packages/sim/src/economy/buildings-data.ts`.)*

- [ ] **Stand-ins F65 left for later features [DEFAULT]** (`data/market.json`, `data/pops.json`, `data/buildings.json` → `standIns`; section 8.6): **the rest of the economy** buys what each market had left over at the start (per person, less when dearer) until the building catalog covers the whole economy (*F19 split off the exports*); **housing, services and luxuries** have no good and are paid at F06's price level (services, construction and luxury goods buildings, and housing **F142**); the **expected standard of living** is the starting wealth less 5 levels (Victoria 3 uses strata and literacy: **F10**, **F118**); the **shortage penalty** (up to 10 points of standard of living) is small for rich pops, since food is a small part of their package (*F08: starvation now hurts through food security, births, deaths and up to 15 more points; unrest comes with F10*); **dividends** go to the province's owners (corporations and shareholders: **F89**; taxes: **F09**); ~~standing imports~~ (*replaced by trade in F19*); the **price history** isn't saved (a year at most, the player's country only); no **local prices** or market access within a country (transport, **F86**).
